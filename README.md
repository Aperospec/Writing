# Writing Craft

Chinese and English writing, editing and translation.

This is an independent, project-neutral skill. Its entry point is [SKILL.md](skills/writing-craft/SKILL.md). Install the complete `skills/writing-craft` directory, including its references. It can be used directly; no ECD studio, producer or other department is a prerequisite.

Project objectives, audiences, styles, formats, research conclusions, operational targets and permissions belong to the project and are supplied per task. They must not become defaults in this skill or its supporting files.

Version 1.2.0-alpha connects material development, explanation, argument, narrative exploration, organization and revision through optional methods drawn from writing textbooks. It expands Chinese sentence and discourse diagnosis while preserving the existing English, translation, narrative and editorial methods. Source notes distinguish complete-book reading from partial chapters and state their limits.

The September 2026 foundation review added complete Chinese and English textbook reading to the documented basis. Existing and candidate instructions both completed four limited writing tasks without a clear difference in outcome, so the candidates were not adopted. This documentation update retains the 1.2.0-alpha methods and version; reading completion is not evidence of general performance improvement.

This independent edition is an alpha. Structural validation and limited forward tasks establish initial evidence, not comprehensive mastery of every discipline named in its scope.
